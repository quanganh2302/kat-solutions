# Product brief

## Purpose

Kat Solutions is a B2B software company website. It demonstrates solutions to factory owners and business owners who want to digitize, automate, or better manage production and operations.

The primary conversion goal is a contact request. In V1 the contact page is visual only; email delivery will be added later.

## Solution domains

- ERP and CRM
- Web platforms and business applications
- AI and process automation, including factory applications/RPA
- Computer Vision
- Data and integration appear as supporting capabilities in the homepage ecosystem

## V1 scope

- Vietnamese is the default language, with an English translation for each public page
- Four page types: home, projects, about, contact; each is available at `/vi/...` and `/en/...`
- Static presentation content and placeholder demo links
- Figma-matched desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts

## Out of scope for V1

- Email submission, chatbot, database, authentication, CMS, analytics, and real demo links

## Content integrity

Use only approved text and case studies from the Figma design or material supplied by the owner. Never fabricate client names, numerical results, testimonials, or integration claims.
